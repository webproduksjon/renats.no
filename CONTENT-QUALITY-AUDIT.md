# Content quality and spam-policy audit

Reviewed against Google's current Search Essentials guidance on people-first content, doorway abuse, scaled content abuse, scraping, and misleading functionality.

## Page classes

### Indexable commercial pages

- `/` — first-party offer, audience, boundaries, founder context and next steps.
- `/tjenester/` — service selection and scope; not a keyword doorway.
- `/tjenester/landingsside.html` — one offer type and when it is appropriate.
- `/tjenester/enkel-nettside.html` — a small business website as a distinct deliverable.
- `/tjenester/nettside-for-sma-bedrifter.html` — a larger but still bounded site option.
- `/bransjer/` — browseable industry hub.
- `/bransjer/elektriker/` — electrician-specific customer journey, safety/documentation context and decision checklist.
- `/bransjer/snekker/` — snekker/tømrer terminology, project preparation and scope questions.
- `/bransjer/rorlegger/` — urgent versus planned plumbing journey and contact information needs.
- `/prosess.html` — delivery, ownership, handover and AI-assisted workflow disclosure.
- `/priser.html` — actual starting prices, inclusions, exclusions and revision policy.
- `/om.html` — named founder, location context, company identity and suitability.
- `/kontakt.html` — enquiry form, direct email and expected next step.
- `/gratis-nettsidekonsept.html` — a concrete, honest offer with limits; not a fake portfolio page.

### Intentionally excluded from Search

- `/blogg/` and unfinished resource article shells — noindex until a complete, original article exists.
- `/ressurser/` — noindex until useful resources are actually written.
- `/prosjekter/` — noindex until real or clearly labeled concept work is available.
- `/bransjer/maler/` and `/bransjer/konsulent/` — noindex until independently researched and written.
- `/personvern.html` — noindex because it is a legal/support page, not a search landing page.
- `/404.html` — noindex error page.

## Findings

- No cloaking or hidden text found.
- No scraped or syndicated body content found.
- No fake reviews, client names, results, certifications or case studies found.
- No multiple city pages or duplicate regional domains found.
- The three industry pages share a conversion purpose, but their substantive customer journeys, terminology, examples and visitor tools differ. Shared navigation and honest scope guidance are normal site architecture, not doorway abuse.
- Unfinished pages are excluded from Search rather than being used as keyword placeholders.
- AI is disclosed as an assisting tool on the process page; facts and client claims remain subject to human/client review.

## Rule for future pages

Do not publish an industry, city, service or article page only to capture a keyword. Publish it only when a visitor who arrives directly would receive an original, complete answer and a useful next step.
