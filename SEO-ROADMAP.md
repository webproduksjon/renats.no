# SEO roadmap for renats.no

This is the working order for building the site without creating thin or doorway-like pages. The route shells for future industries, projects, and resources are intentionally `noindex` until their content is genuinely complete.

## Stage 1 — Positioning and core commercial pages

Finish the homepage, service pages, process, pricing, about, and contact pages. Establish the exact customer, offer, scope, limitations, and conversion path. Do not expand the site until these pages explain the business clearly and honestly.

## Stage 2 — Research-led industry pages

Choose a small number of industries and research their real customer journeys, services, trust signals, terminology, photos, questions, and website needs. Build each industry page as a distinct resource with original examples—not as a word-swapped template.

## Stage 3 — Proof, demos, and conversion assets

Add real projects, permission-based before/after examples, clearly labeled concept demos, and the free homepage-concept offer. Keep demos and mockups useful for visitors; do not invent clients, reviews, results, or case-study claims.

## Stage 4 — Helpful resources and articles

Publish a limited set of articles that answer specific questions for small businesses. Each article needs a clear search intent, original explanation, useful examples, and a deliberate link to the most relevant service or industry page. Avoid publishing articles only because a keyword exists.

## Stage 5 — Technical release and measurement

Once pages are complete, move finished URLs into the sitemap, remove `noindex` where appropriate, validate canonical URLs and HTTP statuses, add suitable structured data, test mobile performance and accessibility, connect Google Search Console, submit the sitemap, and measure both search performance and qualified enquiries.

## Rules for every future page

- One primary purpose and search intent per URL.
- Unique title, description, H1, introduction, and internal-link role.
- Useful content visible in the initial HTML.
- Original research, examples, screenshots, or experience where relevant.
- No fake testimonials, businesses, addresses, reviews, traffic claims, or results.
- No city/industry pages that only swap a word or funnel users to one generic page.
- Do not add a URL to the sitemap until it is complete and worth visiting.
