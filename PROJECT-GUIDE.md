# Guide for future project and proof pages

## Project page types

### Real project
Use only when the work was actually delivered. Record the client’s permission, the exact scope, the URL or screenshots that may be shown, and which claims the client has approved.

### Permission-based before/after
Show the original state only with permission. Explain the specific changes—structure, readability, mobile layout, content hierarchy, or contact path. Do not imply ranking, traffic, conversion, or revenue improvements unless measured and approved.

### Concept demo
Mark the page as **Konsept**, **Demo**, or **Utarbeidet eksempel** above the fold and in the title. Do not use a real business name, logo, review, address, certification, or customer result unless you have permission and the statement is true.

## Required project-page structure

1. Status label: `Reelt prosjekt`, `Konseptdemo`, or `Før/etter med tillatelse`.
2. Who the example is for and what problem the page addresses.
3. Starting point and scope.
4. What was changed or proposed.
5. Relevant screenshots or images with descriptive alt text.
6. What is not shown or not measured.
7. A next step relevant to the visitor.

## Publishing gate

Keep a project URL out of the sitemap and use `noindex` until it has enough original information to be worth visiting. Never use a placeholder project page as an SEO page.
