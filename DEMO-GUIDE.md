# Guide for future concept demos

Concept demos are conversion assets first. They should help a visitor imagine a useful website, not pretend to be evidence of a completed client project.

## Every demo should state

- `Konseptdemo — ikke en ekte kundeside`
- Which industry or business situation it illustrates
- Which assumptions were made
- Which parts would change for a real company
- That images, names, reviews, and claims are illustrative unless explicitly approved

## Make a demo useful

Show the reasoning behind the page: the visitor’s likely question, the recommended service hierarchy, the contact path, the content that would be needed, and what should be verified before publication.

## Avoid

- Fake testimonials
- Fake project results
- Fake addresses or opening hours
- Borrowed logos or screenshots without permission
- Claims that the concept produced traffic or customers
- “Before” screenshots of another company without consent
